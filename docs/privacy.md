# Privacy Policy for NAIIVE

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-01

## 1. Overview

NAIIVE is a local scientific data analysis and visualization application.
NAIIVE does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

---

## 2. Data Collection

NAIIVE:
- Does not collect personal information
- Does not collect device identifiers
- Does not collect usage analytics
- Does not use cookies
- Does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos
- Does not transmit any data to external servers
- Does not connect to the internet

All data processed by NAIIVE remains entirely on your device.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

NAIIVE does not use:
- Analytics services
- Advertising SDKs
- Crash reporting services
- Cloud storage services
- External APIs

---

## 4. Local File Access

NAIIVE may access files that you explicitly open or import within the app.
These files remain stored locally on your device and are not transmitted.

---

## 5. Data Security

Because NAIIVE does not collect or transmit any data, there is no server-side data storage or processing.

All processing occurs locally on your device.

---

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If the app’s functionality changes in the future (e.g., adding analytics, cloud sync, or network features), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
